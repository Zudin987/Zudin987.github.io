BPSR Android DPSMeter Relay

Connect your phone to a PC combat meter over your home network. The Windows manager guides relay setup while you continue playing BPSR on Android.

What it does

  • Guided phone setup with a QR profile for SFA (sing-box for Android).
  • Works with compatible meters that can parse BPSR traffic from the StarSEA process.
  • Separate Home, Details and Help views for everyday use and troubleshooting.

Quick start

  1. Extract the full release ZIP and open BPSR Relay Manager.exe on your PC.
  2. Choose the PC’s LAN address. Follow Prepare Relay, Allow Firewall and Start Phone Setup; import the profile in SFA and route BPSR only.
  3. Start Relay on the PC, start SFA on the phone, then open BPSR. Select StarSEA in your PC DPS meter.

Requirements

  • Windows PC and Android phone on the same trusted LAN
  • SFA on Android
  • A separate compatible PC DPS meter
  • Administrator approval for the Windows firewall rule

Before you use it

This is a relay, not a combat meter. Choose StarSEA as the capture target, not BPSRMobileFront.

The phone-to-PC connection is authenticated but not encrypted. Keep it on a trusted private network and do not port-forward port 10808.
