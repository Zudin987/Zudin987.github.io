BPSR MIDI Lite / Studio

Play songs on Blue Protocol: Star Resonance instruments, solo or with a band. Use Lite for MIDI playback, or Studio to turn an audio file into an arrangement.

What it does

  • Browse local MIDI and Online Sequencer songs, then check notes against your instrument and unlocked category.
  • Use shared Band Mode playback across Lite and Studio.
  • Studio accepts local audio and can arrange Piano, Guitar, Bass and Drums for review and export.

Quick start

  1. Download your chosen edition from Releases and open its EXE. No separate Python installation is needed.
  2. Select your BPSR instrument and unlocked category, choose a song, then review Song Check.
  3. Press Play and return to the game’s instrument screen before the countdown ends. Press F10 to stop.

Requirements

  • 64-bit Windows and BPSR
  • Internet for online songs and first-time Studio setup
  • Additional disk space and processing time for Studio’s models

Before you use it

Playback sends instrument key presses to the game. It does not modify game files.

Studio transcription is experimental. Review the result; dense songs can still contain incorrect or missing notes. Only process audio you have permission to use.
