Quick start

  1. Install the correct APK. Most recent Android phones use arm64-v8a.
  2. Open BlueMeter Lite and tap Start DPS Meter.
  3. Allow Display over other apps and approve Android's VPN prompt.
  4. Open BPSR. The meter appears over the game.

Choose APK

Use arm64-v8a unless you know your device needs another architecture.

arm64-v8a Recommended for most recent phones↓ armeabi-v7a Older 32-bit ARM devices↓ x86_64 Mostly emulators or uncommon x86 devices↓

Notes

  • The Android VPN prompt is used for local packet capture. It is not a remote VPN service.
  • Combat data and encounter history are processed locally on the device.
  • The overlay can show damage, healing and damage received.
  • Game or protocol updates can require a BlueMeter update.
