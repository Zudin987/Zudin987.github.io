Read this first: the method temporarily modifies a BPSR client file. It is unofficial, not supported by the game, and is not guaranteed ban-safe. Game updates or policy changes may also break the method.

Beginner workflow

The app keeps the risky parts visible

  1. Find your BPSR installationUse automatic detection or choose the StreamingAssets\container folder manually.
  2. Choose and crop your imageSelect square profile picture or tall card, then move/zoom the crop until it looks right.
  3. Prepare Guild Photo BoothOpen BPSR, enter Guild Photo Booth and use Windowed mode. The app shows the exact confirmations it needs.
  4. Apply the temporary pictureThe app validates the package and creates an install-specific clean backup before replacing the live file.
  5. Take and save the photo in BPSRUse the square/card resize helpers and save the portrait/card before restoring.
  6. Restore the original BPSR fileThe app refuses unsafe restores when the live file no longer matches a modification it knows it installed.

Safety behavior

Designed to fail closed

  • Backups are separated for different BPSR installations/regions.
  • Interrupted Apply operations can be detected and recovered.
  • Unity bundle compression is preserved when supported.
  • The live package is validated before replacement.
  • The app refuses to force an old backup over an unknown or updated live file.
If BPSR updates while the custom file is active: do not force an old backup over the update. Close the game, use the launcher/Steam Verify or Repair function, then reopen the app.

Privacy

Your selected image stays local

Selected images, crops, hashes and backups remain on your PC. The app does not ask for your BPSR login credentials.
