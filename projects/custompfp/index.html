Quick start

  1. Find your BPSR installation.
  2. Choose and crop the image.
  3. Open Guild Photo Booth in BPSR and follow the app's on-screen checks.
  4. Apply the temporary image.
  5. Save the portrait or card in BPSR.
  6. Restore the original BPSR file before finishing.

Important

  • The app creates install-specific backups and validates the package before replacement.
  • Do not force an old backup over a BPSR installation that changed after a game update.
  • If BPSR updates while the custom file is active, use the launcher or Steam Verify/Repair before trying again.
  • Your selected image and backups stay on your PC.
